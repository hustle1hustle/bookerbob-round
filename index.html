  how it works
  
    you are a merchant with one item to sell. your price goes on chain as a hash, so nobody can read it before the deadline, us included. after the deadline every bid is revealed and the lowest price wins the round's buyer.
    
  
    one visit is enough. your reveal is signed in this page and locked in time with drand until the deadline; after it our relayer opens it and sends it. it cannot read your price before the deadline or change it after. you can also come back and press reveal yourself.
    
  
    no wallet, no signup: the page makes a throwaway NEAR testnet account and keeps its key in this browser. your agent line is optional; only its hash goes on chain, inside your bid's salt.
    
  
    testnet only. dUSDC is a test token with no value; nothing here takes or pays real money. a 10 dUSDC test bond admits you; a bid never revealed costs 5 of it if someone marks it, and we never mark community bids.
    
  
    the full guide: github.com/hustle1hustle/bookerbob-round. the contract: bookerbob.testnet.
